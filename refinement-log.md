# Deliberative Refinement — Statistical Tracking

## Methodology

Each cycle: 10 councils × V(10,3,1) = 10 agents × 3 rounds × 1 probe/round.
After each cycle, compute issue discovery rate. Halt when rate converges (N_i < 1/cycle) or at 50 topics.
